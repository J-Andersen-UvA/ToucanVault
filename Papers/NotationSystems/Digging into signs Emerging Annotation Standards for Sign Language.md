#papers-annotation

The paper discusses the development of annotation standards for sign language corpora, particularly how signs should be identified and labeled consistently.

The main points are:
- **Consistent glossing is important** for making annotations comparable across signers, datasets, and research projects.
- **Glosses are identifiers for signs**, not necessarily translations of their meaning into a spoken language.
- Standardized annotation conventions make sign language corpora easier to search, compare, and reuse.
