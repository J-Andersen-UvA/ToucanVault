#papers-transitions

The paper investigates **kinematic differences between lexical sign movements and transitional movements** in Sign Language of the Netherlands (NGT), using motion capture.

The main points are:
- **Motion capture:** Uses a CyberGlove and Flock of Birds tracking system to record finger movements and 3D hand positions.
- **Movement analysis:** Examines changes in the X, Y, and Z coordinates of the index fingertip.
- **Two NGT signers:** Participants produced sequences of sign pairs, allowing lexical and transitional movements to be compared.
- **Main result: Transitional movements have lower velocity than lexical movements**, on average. <span class="red">Goes against the</span> [[Signs and Transitions Do They Differ Phonetically and Does It Matter]]
- **Segmentation relevance:** Velocity differences may help distinguish lexical movements from transitions in continuous signing.
- **Limitation:** The analysis focuses on fingertip movement rather than directly comparing individual finger-joint rotations or handshape transitions.
