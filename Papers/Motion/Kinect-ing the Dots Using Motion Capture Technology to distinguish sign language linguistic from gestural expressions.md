#papers-motion-analysis

The paper investigates **whether motion capture can distinguish lexical signs from more gestural forms of expression** in Israeli Sign Language (ISL), based on differences in their movement characteristics.

The main points are:
- **Three types of expression:** Compares lexical signs (conventionalized signs), classifier constructions (representing objects, locations, and movements), and constructed action (using the body to enact events or actions).
- **Motion capture analysis:** Uses Microsoft Kinect motion capture technology to quantitatively analyze movements during signing.
- **Kinematic differences:** Finds significant differences in movement characteristics between lexical signs and the other two types of expression.
- **Linguistic vs. gestural movement:** Lexical signs have more constrained, conventionalized movement patterns, while classifier constructions and constructed action allow more gradient, expressive movement.
- **Classifier constructions:** Occupy an intermediate position between conventionalized lexical signs and more gestural constructed action.
- **Main conclusion:** Motion characteristics can help distinguish linguistic categories within continuous signing, although not all signing movements are lexical signs.
