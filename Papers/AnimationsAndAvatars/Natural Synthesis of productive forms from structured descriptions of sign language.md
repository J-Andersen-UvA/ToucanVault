#papers-animation

The paper investigates **how to generate natural sign language animations from structured linguistic descriptions**, particularly for productive signs such as geometric depictions and spatial descriptions.

The main points are:
- **AZee notation:** Uses structured linguistic descriptions to generate animations with the Paula signing avatar.
- **Productive signing:** Focuses on signs constructed dynamically to express spatial relationships and geometric information, rather than retrieving predefined signs.
- **Transitions are important:** Movements between signs are not necessarily meaningless. They can contribute to the linguistic structure and naturalness of signing.
- **Motion capture analysis:** Studies a recorded discourse and corresponding motion capture to understand how movements should be represented and animated.
- **Discourse-level synthesis:** Extends beyond isolated signs to sequences of connected clauses, accounting for how movements relate across the discourse.
- **Main conclusion:** Natural sign language synthesis requires modeling the movement between traditionally annotated signs, not just the signs themselves.
