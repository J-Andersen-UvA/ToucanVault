#papers-animation #papers-postprocessing

The paper proposes a method for editing motion-captured Japanese Sign Language (JSL) using HamNoSys notation, allowing existing sign animations to be modified without recording new motion capture data.

The main points are:
- **Combines motion capture with HamNoSys** to preserve natural movements while allowing modifications.
- **Handshape, hand orientation, and location can be edited** independently using HamNoSys components.
- **Contextual modifications** of signs can be generated from existing recordings, rather than capturing every variation separately.
- The method can generate new movements while retaining natural human-like motion.
